Community updates

Society notices

Important updates to help keep our community clean, safe, comfortable and running smoothly. Please take a moment to read the details.

10 community notices
CLEANING12 Nov 2026

Keep common areas clean

Please do not leave footwear, boxes or personal belongings in corridors and stairwells. Housekeeping will clean shared areas every morning; help us keep walkways clear and hygienic.

WASTE MANAGEMENT13 Nov 2026

Separate wet and dry waste

Residents are requested to place wet and dry waste in separate, closed bags and use the marked collection bins. Please do not leave garbage outside your door or beside the lift.

PARKING14 Nov 2026

Use your designated parking space

Please park only in your allocated slot and keep driveways, ramps and fire access lanes clear at all times. Avoid honking inside the premises, especially during late hours.

WATER16 Nov 2026

Conserve water in daily use

Please report leaking taps, pipes or flush tanks to the management office promptly. Avoid washing balconies or vehicles with running water so our shared supply is used responsibly.

LIFT SAFETY17 Nov 2026

Lift maintenance and safe use

Scheduled lift inspection will take place from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Please use the other lift during this period. Do not overload lifts or allow children to operate them unattended.

ELECTRICITY18 Nov 2026

Save electricity in common areas

Please switch off lights and fans when leaving shared rooms, and report faulty switches or lights to the facility team. Do not connect personal appliances to common-area sockets.

SECURITY19 Nov 2026

Help keep visitor entry secure

Please inform the security desk about expected guests and delivery personnel. Do not share access codes, and ensure the entrance gate closes fully after you enter or exit.

NOISE20 Nov 2026

Observe quiet hours

Please keep music, television and renovation noise low between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. Notify neighbours in advance about any permitted repair work that may cause disruption.

PETS & GARDENS21 Nov 2026

Care for our gardens and shared spaces

Please keep pets on a leash in common areas and clean up after them. Kindly avoid plucking plants, littering the garden or allowing children to damage shared landscaping.

FIRE SAFETY22 Nov 2026

Keep fire exits and equipment accessible

Never store items in staircases, fire exits or in front of fire extinguishers. Please familiarise your household with the nearest exit and report any safety equipment that needs attention.

Have a concern or maintenance request? Please contact the society management office or your building representative so the right team can help.
